    Reading this screen

    No FEC
    Plain packets. Any lost packet damages its frame.
    Fixed FEC
    A constant number of parity packets on every frame.
    Adaptive FEC
    Parity follows the loss the receiver measures, plus a safety margin.
    k and r
    A frame is split into k data packets; r parity packets are added. Any k of the k + r rebuild it.
